Three industries where seeing accurately changes the economics.

Every project starts from the same place: a camera or a depth sensor, and a decision someone currently makes by hand. We build the model that makes that decision repeatable, then hand over software that runs in your environment.

Industrial inspection

Measure area, volume and colour and catch visual defects as parts come down the line — without removing anything or pausing production. The scan completes, the software flags the deformation, the line keeps moving.

  • Defect detection
  • Dimensional measurement
  • Surface & colour analysis

Retail & sizing

Body measurements accurate enough to manufacture against, taken with a phone. Use them for made-to-measure production, to cut return rates on online orders, or to fit eyewear and apparel virtually in real time.

  • Body & face measurement
  • Virtual try-on
  • Eyewear fitting

Healthcare

Deep-learning work developed alongside clinical and care partners, including Deep Therapy — software that lets practitioners hold a conversation with people ordinary contact can no longer reach.

  • Clinical imaging
  • Therapeutic software
  • Synthetic media research

Products we built, and now license.

Each one started as client work and became a standalone product. All of them are in use today.

weScan

wescan.io

Capture, walk through and share a real space in 3D using the LiDAR sensor in an iPhone or iPad. Built for estate agents, architects and anyone who needs a room on record rather than in photographs.

FairFake

fairfake.ai

Consent-based synthetic video for media, entertainment and government, plus seminars on how deepfakes are produced and how they are detected. Work that has been covered by NRC, NOS and VPRO Tegenlicht.

Deep Therapy

deeptherapy.ai

A platform for practitioners to speak with people they cannot otherwise reach, through self-controlled video animation over a normal video call.

Visual Inspection

3duniversum.com

Scanning and analysis software for the production line, deployable on existing camera hardware.

Mobile Scanner

3duniversum.com

Turn a handheld device into a 3D capture rig, with reconstruction handled off-device.

From first conversation to something running.

Most engagements take this shape. The intake is free, and it ends with a straight answer about whether AI is the right tool for the problem you brought us.

01

Learn

We sit down with your team, look at the problem and the data you already have, and map out what is actually solvable.

02

Plan

A step-by-step plan with scope, timeline and the measure we will hold the result against before anyone writes code.

03

Implement

We build, test against that measure, and deliver working software — with your engineers alongside ours if you want to take it over.
